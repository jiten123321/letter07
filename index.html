Ladoo,

Nothing happened today. No date, no occasion, nothing I need to make up for. I woke up, remembered you exist, and the rest of the day just went well. I didn't know a whole day could turn on something that small.

People will tell you you have pretty eyes. They're right, but they're only seeing the easy part. I like them for what happens right before you laugh, when you've already decided something is funny and you're just waiting for everyone else to catch up. That's the part I'd keep.

And I know that when your mood goes off, you don't say anything. You go quiet. I also know that what you need right then isn't someone explaining things to you. You need to be fed. Two bites in and you laugh at something on your own, like nothing ever happened. The whole world will keep trying to talk you out of it. I'll just set a plate down.

We're opposite in most things. You're coffee, I'm chai. You say it out loud, I think about it for three days. None of that has ever broken anything. We don't match, we fit — and those are two different things.

A hundred roses is a ridiculous number. That's the point. There was no reason for any of this, and you're the reason anyway.

These won't wilt. Neither will the rest of it.
